Quick rules

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard.
  • Land on rivals to send them back, unless the tile is safe ⭐.
  • Roll the exact number needed to enter the center home.
  • A roll of 6 gives an extra turn. Three 6s in one turn skips the turn.
